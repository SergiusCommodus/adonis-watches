Privacy notice

Your privacy

Adonis is a venture of Carlisle Capital LLC. This notice explains what we collect through this site and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the list or reserve a watch, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, email address, the watch you are interested in, an optional preferred number, wrist size and note.

How we use it

We use your details only to manage your reservation and to email you about Adonis, such as prototype news, pre order dates and pricing. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by our form provider, which stores them on our behalf. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your details, or unsubscribe at any time, by replying to any email we send you.

Changes

We will update this notice if anything changes, and post the new version here.
