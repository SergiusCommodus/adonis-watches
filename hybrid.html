Mission Hybrid

A true chronograph above. A health companion below.

Mission Hybrid looks, feels and reads like a classic chronograph. Underneath, a sensor caseback quietly tracks your heart, sleep and recovery, and the hands never ask you to charge them.

Join the Hybrid listWhat it tracks

What it tracks

Everything that matters.
Nothing that glows.

Heart rate

Measured continuously through the caseback, day and night, with resting rate and range.

Blood oxygen

Spot checks and overnight readings, so you can see how well you breathe as you sleep.

HRV and recovery

Heart rate variability turned into a simple read on how ready you are to train.

Sleep

Duration, stages and a nightly score, without a screen glowing on your wrist.

Skin temperature

Small shifts from your baseline, an early hint that something is off.

Silent alerts

A gentle vibration for calls and messages. Glance at your phone only when it matters.

The caseback

Sensors where
they belong.

Optical sensors sit flush in the caseback, against your skin, where readings are most reliable. The dial stays exactly what it should be: a beautiful watch.

  • Real hands, real dial
  • Hands run on their own cell
  • Time set from your phone
  • No screen, ever

How it works

Wear it. Live. Check in.

Wear it

Put it on like any fine watch. Pair it once with the Adonis app and the time sets itself.

It reads, quietly

While you work, train and sleep, the caseback measures what your body is doing. No buzzing, no glow.

Check in

Open the app when you want to: sleep score, recovery, heart trends, steps and workouts.
