Questions

Good to know

What does reserving mean?

It puts you on the Adonis list for the model you choose. No payment is taken. When pre orders open, people on the list hear first, in the order they reserved.

When will watches ship?

Mission I is planned for 2027. Mission Hybrid is in development. Dates can move as prototypes are tested, and we will keep everyone on the list updated.

Can I choose my number?

You can request a preferred caseback number from 1 to 200 for Mission I. Requests are matched in reservation order wherever possible.

What will it cost?

Mission I is planned at $279 to $299, and Mission Hybrid is expected at $399 to $429. Final prices are confirmed before pre orders open.

Does Mission Hybrid need charging?

The hands run on their own quartz cell, so the watch always tells time. The health sensors use a rechargeable battery, and the app lets you know when it is running low.

Is Adonis affiliated with a space agency or another watchmaker?

No. Adonis is an independent brand inspired by the golden age of spaceflight and by ancient Greece. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any space agency or watch company.
