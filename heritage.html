Heritage

Where Apollo’s laurel meets the Moon.

Two stories carry the same name. One is a god of light, music and victory. The other is the voyage that carried people to the Moon. Adonis is built where they meet.

I

The name

Adonis.

In Greek myth, Adonis was the mortal so beautiful that the gods themselves quarreled over him.

We took the name as a standard to hold ourselves to. A watch should be beautiful first, and everything else should earn its place behind the dial.

II

The laurel

Time, earned.

The laurel was sacred to Apollo, and the Greeks crowned their victors with it.

It sits on every Adonis dial, on every crown, and on the shield of the hoplite who opens this site. It stands for effort that is rewarded.

III

The mission

The golden age of spaceflight.

Apollo also lent his name to humanity's greatest voyage. The Mission series is our tribute to that era: chronographs with tachymeter bezels, domed crystals, and the surface of the Moon pressed into every caseback.

Each future edition will honor a moment from the age of exploration, numbered and few.

IV

The hoplite

Discipline over spectacle.

The hoplite was the citizen soldier of ancient Greece. He stood shoulder to shoulder with his neighbors, shield to shield, and held the line.

That is the spirit of Mission Hybrid: a watch that quietly helps you train, recover and sleep, without ever asking you to stare at a screen.

What we stand for

Three principles.

Original by design

Every dial, crown, caseback and mark is drawn for Adonis. Inspired by history, never borrowed from it.

Honest by default

We state specifications plainly, and mark what is preliminary as preliminary. You will always know what you are buying.

Numbered and few

Small editions with numbered casebacks, so every Adonis is one of a known few.

Adonis is an independent brand. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any space agency or watch company.
