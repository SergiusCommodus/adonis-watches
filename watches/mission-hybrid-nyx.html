The story

Named for the goddess of the night. A black and gold chronograph on supple black leather, with a sensor caseback that quietly reads your heart rate, sleep and recovery. Real hands. No screen. Ever.

Timekeeping runs on its own quartz cell, so the hands never stop, even when the sensors need a charge.

Specifications
Case40 mm stainless steel, polished and brushed
TimekeepingQuartz, set automatically from your phone
SensorsOptical heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, motion
TracksHeart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, sleep, steps, workouts
AlertsSilent vibration for calls and messages
BatteryHands run on their own cell. Sensors recharge, target of up to 10 days
ConnectivityBluetooth, Adonis app for iPhone and Android
Water resistance50 m
StrapBlack calfskin with contrast stitching

Preliminary specifications. Final figures are confirmed before production.
