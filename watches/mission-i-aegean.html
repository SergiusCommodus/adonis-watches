The story

Named for the sea the ancient Greeks crossed, Aegean pairs a sunray blue dial with crisp silver indices and a matching blue tachymeter bezel. Polished links, a domed crystal, and the surface of the Moon pressed into its back.

Like every Mission I, it carries a lunar caseback and is part of a numbered first edition of 200.

Specifications
Case40 mm stainless steel, polished and brushed
MovementMeca quartz chronograph
FunctionsHours, minutes, small seconds, chronograph, tachymeter
CrystalDomed sapphire with anti reflective coating
CasebackLunar relief, individually numbered
Water resistance100 m
